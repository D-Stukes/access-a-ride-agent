## The front end (v1 design, everything we agreed on — keep all of it)
Single file: `frontend/index.html` (vanilla HTML/CSS/JS). Images are in `frontend/assets/`.
1. **Video scene:** a silent strip of 4 NYC Access-A-Ride YouTube clips.
   - We **removed** two clips: the MTA "MY AAR" app video (blue screen), and an RTD Denver promo ("Trip Cost", the booking page, the map). Don't bring them back.
2. **After 5 seconds, "The Problem" fades in over the video:**
   - Partly see-through dark boxes with text shadows, so the video stays visible.
   - The 82-year-old dialysis story.
   - **"3 Pain Points"**: late or no-show driver; no call-out and assist; driver leaves early, so the rider is stranded and marked a "No Show".
   - Stats: 72,823 complaints (Sep 2025–Aug 2026); 20,315 no-shows (28%); 0 vendors named. Source: NY Open Data.
3. **Click → "Welcome to Ride Assure"**, a title phone only, with no active buttons:
   - "Welcome / to / Ride Assure", the van logo, and a mint "Voice help for paratransit riders" tag.
   - A black-and-white wheelchair silhouette picture.
   - Talking-point cards on each side.
4. **Try It (`#demos`):** the working demo phone, centered on photos that cross-fade every 10 seconds. The tagline floats away.
   - The selected option turns red.
   - The app repeats the request back.
   - It "calls" Paratransit Dispatch 877-337-2017 and plays my recording `assets/aar-answer.m4a`.
   - It says "Please hold while the agent is processing your request", then "Call is now completed", then the answer card.
   - It also has **AAR Links** (in-phone browser with a red X) and a **Trip Complaint List** at the bottom of the options.
   - The call-out request reads "Please tell the driver to come and perform call out and assist. I need help." It logs "The driver did not call out and assist" to the complaint list.
5. **Team section and footer.**
   - Nav: Home · Try It · The Problem · Team.
   - Logo: a flipped van with a yellow-and-navy check badge (`ride-assure-logo.png`), also the browser-tab icon.
   - Hidden sections (still in the file): Architecture, the agent chat, and the Try the Agent button.
- **Clicker keys:** the right arrow moves forward, the left arrow moves back.
- **Phone layout:** an `@media (max-width:700px)` block. The desktop view is unchanged.
- The colors are light (no heavy dark themes), with a navy and yellow accent. Every option is read aloud with a nice voice (Google US English or Samantha).
