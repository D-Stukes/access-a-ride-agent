The problem

Missed pickups go unrecorded

Imagine you're 82, you use a walker, and you have a dialysis appointment at ten. Your van is scheduled for 9:15. It shows up at 9:50, the driver doesn't come to the door, and leaves two minutes later. You're marked a no-show.

3 Pain Points

  • Driver comes late or doesn't show up
  • Driver does not perform call out and assist
  • Driver leaves early, causing the patient to be stranded and listed as a “No Show”
72,823

complaints logged by NYC paratransit from September 2025 to August 2026

20,315

were “Service – No Shows,” the largest category (28%)

0

vendors named in the public data, so no one can see which company keeps failing riders

Source: NY Open Data, MTA NYCT Paratransit Customer Complaints, queried October 8, 2026.

Ride Assure

The paratransit service already has an app that provides live tracking of the vendor's vehicle and driver on the way to a trip. Ride Assure focuses on the features that are still missing and make the biggest difference for seniors: call-out and assist, accountability, and reporting late arrivals and early departures by the driver, all through plain voice commands that get a real answer fast.

Trip Complaint List

Built in to the app: riders log a late pickup, a missed call-out, or an early departure in one tap. Nothing is saved until they say yes, and the records show patterns by vendor.
