## Things to try that should fail

- Give only a name: the rep asks for the AAR ID.
- Give the right AAR ID with the wrong date of birth: not verified.
- Get it wrong three times: the call is locked and the rep explains where to find your AAR ID.

## About shared rides

The app and the rep only share **counts and order** for other passengers ("5 drop-offs before yours"). They never say other riders' names or show their stops on the map, because that would reveal where other people live.

Times in this sheet are the seeded plan. When the live simulator is built, it will shift today's runs to start when you begin the demo.
