**Flow on the main phone:**
1. The rider taps a ride question.
2. Ride Assure asks to share the AAR ID and date of birth (tap or say yes or no).
3. It rings, and the live call shows inside the phone.
4. Hold: about 20 seconds with smooth jazz, plus a "While you're on hold" screen (switch music or play the matching game). Then "Marisol is back."
5. The answer card shows the ticket number and is read aloud.
6. The "While you wait" button opens a game or music while the van countdown runs. The countdown uses a demo clock (1 minute = 3 seconds) and alerts at 5 minutes and when the van is on the block.

**Which buttons do what:**
- These make the live call: "Is my ride here?", "has not arrived", "remind call out", "come and assist", "few more minutes".
- "Caregiver" still plays the v1 recorded call.
- If the API fails, the v1 recorded call plays instead.

## Gotchas
- **Clicking near the bottom of the phone during a call hits the red End call button** ("You ended the call"). Don't click in the browser window while a call runs.
- **The demo moves Rosa's pickup to now + 15 minutes** on every call. "Today's trip" is now any trip within 12 hours of now. The nightly reset restores the data.
- **Headings collapse on scroll:** `#demos .demo-head` collapses (the `head-gone` class) as you scroll; v2's CSS raises its max-height to 420px.
